Weekly Call-Log Review

ShopDispatch · early access · pricing shared on request

You send your call log once a week. You get back a short note on where calls were lost and what to coach. That’s it.

How it works

  1. Your shop keeps the free Night-Call Log (or the Reason Card) with the Issue #4 reason codes.
  2. Once a week, you send that week’s log. We’ll agree on how when you start.
  3. A person on our side reads every row and sends back a short note: the bookable-but-lost codes that came up most, the Other lines worth a closer look, rows where the fee or capacity check was skipped, and one or two things to coach next week.

Plainly

  • It’s early access. We’re taking a small number of shops and working out the format with them.
  • A human reviews your log. It isn’t an automated report.
  • No guarantees. We don’t promise a booking rate or any revenue number. The note is only as good as the log you keep.
  • Price: early-access pricing shared on request. Nothing is charged by filling in this form.
  • Your data: send only what the log needs. Strip customer names if you like; the codes and notes are what matter.
