Fee Card

ShopDispatch · one page · tape it by the phone

Write your fees down once, from your own written rules, so everyone who answers the phone says the same fee the same way. One page, no software required.

Download the card (PDF, letter size)

How to use it

  1. Fill in section 1 once: your daytime and after-hours fees, what the fee covers, and whether each one comes off the bill.
  2. Fill in a replacement range only if your shop already has one in writing. If not, leave it blank. Blank means no range, so don’t guess.
  3. Tape it by every phone that takes calls. Say the fee once, up front, before any truck promise.
  4. When a call doesn’t book over the fee, log E. When it doesn’t book because they wanted a price first, log F.

What’s on it

  • Our fees. Blanks for each fee, what it covers, and credited yes/no.
  • Say it once. The fee line, one pushback line, and one “still no” line.
  • Price-first callers. Three questions to ask first. Repairs get the fee only, never a repair price. Replacements get a range only if it’s written on the card, followed by an offer of an in-home estimate visit.

The card is our suggestion, not an industry standard. Not legal advice: fee disclosure rules vary by state, so check with your state contractor board or a lawyer before you settle your fee wording.
